## The problem

Admin work often ends with a pile of files that belong together: a scanned receipt, a PDF invoice, a photo of a signed form. Online mergers want you to upload those documents to someone else's server, which is the wrong place for financial paperwork.

## What I built

A web app that merges PDFs and images into one PDF without sending anything anywhere.

- Each page shows as a preview, so you can see what you're merging.
- Pages can be rotated or removed one at a time.
- PDFs and images can be mixed in the same file.

## What changed

Merging documents became a step that takes seconds and keeps the files on the computer they started on.
