## Findings
- #1 low: The video button changes its label (Play/Pause) and also flips `aria-pressed`, so a screen reader announces "Pause, toggle button, pressed" while the video plays. That is a double signal. Drop `aria-pressed` and key the icon CSS on a `data-playing` attribute, the way the hero's Pause animation button works (partials/journey.html:43, journey.scss:1112-1119, journey.js:385).
- #2 low: At 1440, "The whole journey in twelve seconds" breaks as "...in twelve / seconds". Add `text-wrap: balance` to `.jn-intro .block-title`, or to `.block-title` (journey.scss:928).
- #3 low: With no JavaScript or with reduced motion, each step title is a disabled button, so screen readers say "Opens the link on her phone, button, unavailable", which sounds broken. Render the titles as plain text and let journey.js turn them into buttons when motion is allowed, or swap the element in `applyMotionPreference` (partials/journey.html:24).
- #4 low: The "How this page moves" row "Reduced motion" says the page starts from the finished picture, but the hero rests on the chosen amount. Say "the walkthrough shows the finished picture and the hero rests on the chosen amount". Related: the intros say "Scroll to follow" and "It plays while it is on screen", which reduced-motion visitors do not get (donation-website.md:66; partials/journey.html lines 12 and 35).
- #5 low: In dark, the video button has a light `--ink` fill on the always-light poster, so it shows as a pale box with almost no edge. The recording is light in both themes, like the phone, so give the button fixed colours (for example `--jn-bezel` fill with `--jn-screen` text, both theme-independent) or a `--rule-strong` edge (journey.scss:1086).
- #6 low: At 390 the panel's record lines ("DN-2047, Rp150.000, monthly", "Saved") render at 10.35 px, since `clamp(11.5px, ...)` times 0.9em. The panel is the page's payoff and is hard to read on a phone. Raise the floor so the smallest line is at least 12 px, then recheck the panel still fits the room (journey.scss:773).
- #7 low: At 390x844 the Pause animation button starts at y=826, so it is mostly below the first screen while the hero is moving. In the pinned story the second figure breathes and blinks one to two screens away from the button. WCAG 2.2.2 is met (the mechanism is on the page and stops everything), but it is hard to find. Consider placing the button above the scene at narrow widths, or a second Pause in the story stage (work-page.html:46).
